Section 1: Introduction and Plan Overview

BluePeak Health ("the Plan", "we", "us") provides health benefit coverage to eligible members enrolled in the BluePeak Silver Marketplace. This Evidence of Coverage ("EOC") describes the terms and conditions of your coverage, including covered services, cost-sharing obligations, exclusions, and your rights as a member.

ACA marketplace silver-tier plan with cost-sharing reductions, $3,000 individual deductible, and essential health benefits.

Coverage is effective for the plan year beginning January 1, 2025 and ending December 31, 2025, subject to continued eligibility. All benefits are subject to the terms, limitations, and exclusions described in this document.

Section 2: Covered Benefits

The following services are covered when Medically Necessary (as defined in Section 5) and provided by an in-network provider unless otherwise noted:

2.1 Preventive Care

Preventive services as defined by the USPSTF (Grade A or B recommendations), HRSA guidelines for women and children, and immunizations recommended by the ACIP are covered at no cost share when rendered by an in-network provider. Examples include annual wellness visits, immunizations, and cancer screenings.

2.2 Diagnostic Imaging

Diagnostic imaging services including MRI, CT scans, PET scans, and X-rays are covered when ordered by a licensed physician for the diagnosis or treatment of a covered condition. Prior authorization is required for non-emergency MRI and CT procedures (see Section 3). Coverage is limited to medically necessary studies using the most appropriate and lowest-cost modality that achieves the clinical objective.

2.3 Specialist Services

Visits to in-network specialists are covered. Out-of-network specialist services are covered only for emergency conditions or when no in-network provider is reasonably available.

2.4 Mental Health and Substance Use Disorder

Mental health and substance use disorder benefits are provided at parity with medical and surgical benefits in accordance with the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA). Covered services include outpatient therapy, inpatient psychiatric care, intensive outpatient programs, and medication-assisted treatment.

2.5 Emergency Services

Emergency services at any hospital emergency department — in-network or out-of-network — are covered at the in-network cost-sharing level. Balance billing protections apply in accordance with the No Surprises Act (effective January 1, 2022).

2.6 Prescription Drugs

Outpatient prescription drugs are covered under the Plan's Pharmacy Benefit. The formulary is organized into tiers with varying cost-sharing levels. Step therapy or prior authorization may be required for certain medications. Refer to the Pharmacy Benefit Summary for the current formulary.

Section 4: Exclusions and Limitations

The following services are excluded from coverage under this Plan:

  • Services that are not Medically Necessary as defined in Section 5.
  • Cosmetic procedures not required to correct a functional impairment.
  • Experimental or investigational treatments not supported by established clinical evidence.
  • Services rendered by an out-of-network provider, except in emergencies or as otherwise stated.
  • Services rendered without required prior authorization.
  • Custodial care or long-term care not requiring skilled nursing.
  • Services covered under workers' compensation or no-fault auto insurance.
  • Vision and dental services (covered under separate benefit plans, if applicable).
  • Weight-loss programs or bariatric surgery (unless medically necessary per Plan criteria).
  • Fertility treatments beyond the initial diagnostic evaluation (varies by state mandate).

Section 5: Medical Necessity Criteria

"Medically Necessary" means services or supplies that are:

  1. Required for the diagnosis or treatment of an illness, injury, or condition;
  2. Consistent with the symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment of the member's condition;
  3. In accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice;
  4. Not primarily for the convenience of the member or provider; and
  5. The most appropriate level of service that can safely be provided.

The Plan uses nationally recognized clinical guidelines (InterQual, MCG Health) in evaluating medical necessity. Members and providers may request the specific criteria used to make a determination by contacting Member Services.

Section 6: Out-of-Network Coverage

This plan provides coverage primarily for in-network services. Out-of-network services are covered only for emergency care or when the Plan determines that no in-network provider is available within a reasonable distance and timeframe for the needed service.

Section 8: Claims Submission

In-network providers submit claims directly to BluePeak Health. Members using out-of-network providers may need to submit claims within 90 days of the date of service. Failure to file within this period may result in denial of the claim as untimely (Denial Code: CO-29).

Section 9: Grievances and Appeals

If your claim is denied, you have the right to file an internal appeal and, if applicable, request an independent external review.

9.1 Internal Appeal

You must submit a written appeal within 180 days of receiving the denial notice. BluePeak Health will issue a decision within 30 days for post-service claims and 60 days for pre-service claims. Expedited appeal decisions are issued within 72 hours for urgent care situations.

9.2 External Independent Review

After exhausting the internal appeal process, you may request an independent external review through the state-approved Independent Review Organization (IRO) within 30 days of the internal appeal denial. The IRO's decision is binding on BluePeak Health.

9.3 Expedited Review

If your treating physician certifies that the standard appeal timeframe could seriously jeopardize your life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, you may request an expedited appeal. Expedited decisions are issued within 72 hours of receipt.

Section 10: Member Rights and Responsibilities

As a member, you have the right to:

  • Receive information about the Plan, its services, practitioners, and member rights.
  • Be treated with respect and recognition of your dignity and right to privacy.
  • Participate with practitioners in decision-making about your healthcare.
  • A candid discussion of appropriate or medically necessary treatment options.
  • Voice grievances or file appeals about the Plan or care provided.
  • Make recommendations regarding the Plan's member rights and responsibilities policies.

Section 11: Coordination of Benefits

If you are covered under more than one health plan, coordination of benefits (COB) rules determine the order in which plans pay. This Plan uses the "birthday rule" for dependent children and the NAIC COB Model Regulation for other situations. Total payments from all plans will not exceed 100% of covered charges.

Section 12: Continuation Coverage

If you lose coverage under this Plan, you may be eligible for continued coverage under COBRA (if the Plan is subject to COBRA) or state continuation laws. You must elect continuation coverage within 60 days of the qualifying event. Premiums for continuation coverage are the full premium cost plus a 2% administrative fee.
